VET4Hydro (Vocational Education and Training Partnership for Hydropower, 2023–2026), for which I am principal investigator, is an Erasmus+ capacity-building project in vocational education and training (ERASMUS-EDU-2023-CB-VET, grant agreement 101128965), coordinated by LNEC with partners in Portugal, Mozambique, Cabo Verde and Norway. It develops training on hydraulic infrastructures, including modules on GIS and reservoir dimensioning.
