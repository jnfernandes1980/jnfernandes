HydroAI, Lda. is a SaaS start-up spun off from my research at LNEC. It develops a platform for monitoring reservoirs from satellite data, combining:

* water-body mapping from Sentinel-1 (SAR) and Sentinel-2 (optical) imagery
* LSTM-based forecasting of stored volumes
* bathymetric reconstruction
* water-quality monitoring
